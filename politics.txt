As of 2023, some recent actions and policies by the BJP include:

1. **Economic Initiatives:** The government continues to promote infrastructure projects like the GatiShakti master plan, aiming to improve logistics and connectivity. Digital economy and startup ecosystem support remain priorities.

2. **Social Schemes:** Expansion of welfare schemes such as Ujjwala (liquefied petroleum gas connections), PM Awas Yojana (housing), and healthcare initiatives like Ayushman Bharat.

3. **Agricultural and Rural Policies:** Some states have implemented or modified agricultural policies, while the central government emphasizes rural development and farmer welfare through schemes like PM Kisan.

4. **National Security:** Strengthening border security and defense cooperation, particularly with neighboring countries like China and Pakistan, remains a focus, including military modernization.

5. **Cultural and Nationalist Campaigns:** The BJP actively promotes cultural nationalism, supporting policies related to Hindu cultural heritage, and has taken steps to incorporate these themes into education and public discourse.

6. **Legal and Policy Reforms:** Ongoing reforms in banking, labor laws, and digital governance are part of the government's agenda.

7. **Handling Regional Tensions:** Managing regional and ethnic tensions, especially in states like Manipur, Assam, and Jammu & Kashmir, remains a priority, often involving security measures and dialogue.

These actions reflect BJP’s ongoing emphasis on development, nationalism, and governance reform, while also addressing regional and social issues.
